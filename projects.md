VNTA Group
{:.label}

I lead strategy, brand and engineering direction across Vendr, Éirvox and
Maison Seul, on shared infrastructure so each scales on common foundations
while keeping its own identity. Vendr treats the machine as infrastructure
rather than retail. Éirvox is built for discovery and considered buying.
Maison Seul is deliberately quiet, and has not launched.

SpaceXplorer
{:.label}

An analysis of every orbital launch attempt from Sputnik 1 in October 1957 to
2022: 4,630 flights, 62 operators, 370 rockets and 158 launch sites. It found
that the launch rate halved after 1978 and stayed flat for three decades, then
2021 broke the 1971 record with 157 launches. My final year project at the
National College of Ireland, published open source and still up.
